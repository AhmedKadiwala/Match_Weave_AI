# Historical result data license and attribution

The combined normalized dataset and the Wikipedia-derived supplements are distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The source record links and attribution must remain with redistributed data. Changes: structured club/date/regulation-score extraction, normalization, cancellation handling, deduplication and pre-match feature calculation. No article prose, photographs, footage, logos or badges are copied.

Underlying OpenFootball source snapshots are [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original notices are in the five `*-LICENSE.md` files here. `coverage.json` records each source repository, exact commit, URL and SHA-256 hash. Upstream sources: openfootball/football.json, champions-league, espana, italy and europe.

Wikipedia supplements are attributed to **Wikipedia contributors**. Each match has a source URL pinned to a specific article revision. `wikipedia-supplements.json` records titles, revisions, revision-history links, source hashes and licensing. Pages cover the 2025–26 Europa League and Conference League league/knockout phases and their 2026 finals. Article/revision-history links provide contributor attribution. These supplements are CC BY-SA 4.0 and are not relabeled as CC0.

The fitted result-model JSON artifacts are also released under CC BY-SA 4.0 with the same dataset attribution. Original application code, original synthetic events and graphics remain MIT. No source endorses MatchWeave. Coverage counts do not certify official accuracy, and this is not an official live feed.
