# Data and statistical methods

Five fictional 90-minute scenarios; seeds 41, 52, 63, 74, 85. No real football data, branding, footage or player identities. Re-generate: `python -m scripts.generate_data --density 1`. Density range 0.5–3 changes event frequency. Generation is a discrete event simulation, not a physically calibrated tracking simulator. Scoring times are authored narrative anchors. Shots leading to goals have explicit same-player source links and count once. Random other shots cannot become goals.

Pitch coordinates: 105 × 68 metres, normalized so the event's team attacks toward x=105 in both halves. Renderers rotate away-team coordinates for the common pitch. Period 2 begins at 45:00; there is no added time. Ball/player locations between events are not tracked. Initializations, kickoffs and scripted pressing restarts reposition possession; do not infer player speed or distance from event-to-event gaps. Only individual carry distance/duration is physically bounded. Shot speed is a synthetic attribute, not video inference.

Possession = team's pass/carry/shot count divided by both teams' such actions (an event-share proxy, not possession time). Pass completion = completed / attempted passes. Final-third entry = completed pass/carry starting x<70 and ending x≥70. Recovery counts are explicit possession wins. Pressure intensity = pressure events per elapsed minute in the latest ten minutes. Events at the current window lower bound enter current; previous window excludes that boundary. Momentum = weighted shots (5), goals (8), pressures (1.5), recoveries (1), carries (.25), discounted to 35% outside the attacking third, in five-minute bins. It has arbitrary relative units and is not win probability.

Synthetic xG Proxy = clamp(.55 × exp(-distance_to_goal_center/18) × (1 - abs(y-34)/68), .01, .7). Sum over shots. Uncalibrated heuristic; no predictive accuracy claim. Counterattack proxy: possession won at x≤45 then pass/carry/shot reaches x≥70 within 15 s in the same possession ID. Defensive actions do not change possession until an explicit possession_change.

Claim evidence sets exactly equal their independently recounted source events. Fewer than five observations are labeled small sample; five or more are moderate descriptive evidence, not statistical significance. Goals have high confidence as observations, not causal explanations. No causal effect is attributed to substitutions. All UI maps show event locations rather than tracking heatmaps.
